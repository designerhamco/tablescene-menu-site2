## Menu layout boundaries

- Before changing templates, read `docs/template-spacing-contract.md`. PC/tablet single-page fit is one policy; mobile single-page reading is a separate policy with its own numeric tokens. Never let a PC/tablet fit, density, fill or semantic-ratio change alter mobile sizing/spacing.
- The seven active single-page mobile templates share the approved 20px item / 24px heading / 48px section defaults (rem-based). Measure colored upper boxes and separator stroke edges, not just text bounds. Preserve menu order, price/name alignment and desktop notice anchors.
- Display and multi-page engines are excluded from this mobile rollout. All seven mobile templates use 14px notices, 26px category titles and a shared text-brand fit with a hard 44px minimum. Prefer a single line up to 100% width but stop at the width-relative visual ceiling (72px at 342px inner width); balanced multiple lines use a 75% soft target and a 56px ceiling at that inner width. Minimum size, whole-text preservation and visual ceiling take priority over an impossible coverage percentage. No old template-specific title/category/notice size corrections may override these values. Notice rows use normal supporting-copy line-height with zero paragraph gap. Logos bypass text fitting. PC/tablet starts from the three-semantic-field reference; only materially excessive actual whitespace with a safe size gain, or actual wrapped-copy clipping, may switch to actual-content measurement. Fit cache keys include the measurement basis and content/viewport changes restore the reference. Keep readable rem floors (name/price 1rem, supporting copy 0.75rem, secondary name 0.75rem). Store description, product description, all notices and featured product name share one supporting-size token. Keep 1/1.10/1.20 between-item rhythm, template role ratios, insets, category gaps and notice anchors. Prefer registered contiguous category order; only materially better related-category packing may relax it. Unknown families, page boundaries and widgets are order barriers. Prefer up to three menu columns; a fourth is a readability rescue only when its actual inner width is at least 12rem. PC/tablet single-page content NEVER falls back to scrolling, including portrait tablets (768px and wider) and header cells. Attempt safe packing and ordered flow; do not mark clipping, extra offscreen columns or failed fit as ready. Mobile alone retains the independent reading-scroll policy.
- Mobile internal copy gaps are also shared: item title/secondary/description boundaries 2px, category title/description 8px, brand/description 12px, upper content blocks 24px. All supporting copy uses line-height 1.5. Language controls must use script-aware text with the saved/starter Korean and English font variables, never hardcoded font names. Decorative stroke offsets remain distinct from whitespace measured after a stroke.
- PC/tablet text brands use the same slot-based one/two-line comparison for all seven templates: horizontal headers target 60% of the actual central title cell's inner width, vertical brand columns target 75%; both use a common 18% reading-frame height budget and a 15% size-gain threshold for balanced two-line candidates. Horizontal headers reserve 18% of the board inner height after the existing row gap; their title fits the actual fixed row height, so a two-line brand uses smaller text instead of growing the row. The safety floor is 10% of slot width with root text scaling, not device-specific fixed px limits. Do not add template-name-specific title sizes or automatically prioritize one line. Mobile remains on its existing independent policy.
- Verify all affected mobile templates, narrow/wide phone widths and relevant locales, plus PC/tablet and excluded-engine regressions. Record actual DOM viewport dimensions, not just requested sizes; wait for the rendered layout/fonts to settle before claiming success.

- Customer content edits must invalidate fit caches even when IDs/counts stay the same. Recompute after visible copy, prices, images, widgets, fonts or viewport changes. Sale countdown ticks must not invalidate this content signature.

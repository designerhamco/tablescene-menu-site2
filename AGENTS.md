## Menu layout boundaries

- Before changing templates, read `docs/template-spacing-contract.md`. PC/tablet single-page fit is one policy; mobile single-page reading is a separate policy with its own numeric tokens. Never let a PC/tablet fit, density, fill or semantic-ratio change alter mobile sizing/spacing.
- The seven active single-page mobile templates share the approved 20px item / 24px heading / 48px section defaults (rem-based). Measure colored upper boxes and separator stroke edges, not just text bounds. Preserve menu order, price/name alignment and desktop notice anchors.
- Display and multi-page engines are excluded from this mobile rollout. All seven mobile templates use 14px notices, 26px category titles and a shared text-brand fit with a hard 44px minimum. Prefer a single line up to 100% width but stop at the width-relative visual ceiling (64px at 342px inner width); balanced multiple lines use a 75% soft target and a 48px ceiling at that inner width. Minimum size, whole-text preservation and visual ceiling take priority over an impossible coverage percentage. No old template-specific title/category/notice size corrections may override these values. Notice rows use normal supporting-copy line-height with zero paragraph gap. Logos bypass text fitting. PC/tablet starts from the three-semantic-field reference; only materially excessive actual whitespace with a safe size gain, or actual wrapped-copy clipping, may switch to actual-content measurement. Fit cache keys include the measurement basis and content/viewport changes restore the reference. Keep readable rem floors (name/price 1rem, supporting copy 0.75rem, secondary name 0.75rem). Store description, product description, featured description and all notices share one supporting-size token. Featured product name, chip and price must match the corresponding menu name, chip and price size on PC/tablet/mobile; featured names no longer use the supporting-copy size. Keep 1/1.10/1.20 between-item rhythm, template role ratios, insets, category gaps and notice anchors. Prefer registered contiguous category order; only materially better related-category packing may relax it. Unknown families, page boundaries and widgets are order barriers. Prefer up to three menu columns; a fourth is a readability rescue only when its actual inner width is at least 12rem. PC/tablet single-page content NEVER falls back to scrolling, including portrait tablets (768px and wider) and header cells. Attempt safe packing and ordered flow; do not mark clipping, extra offscreen columns or failed fit as ready. Mobile alone retains the independent reading-scroll policy.
- Mobile internal copy gaps are also shared: item title/secondary/description boundaries 2px, category title/description 8px, brand/description 12px, upper content blocks 24px. All supporting copy uses line-height 1.5. Language controls must use script-aware text with the saved/starter Korean and English font variables, never hardcoded font names. Decorative stroke offsets remain distinct from whitespace measured after a stroke.
- PC/tablet text brands compare one/two-line candidates for all seven templates. Horizontal headers target 65% of the actual central title cell's inner width with width-relative 8%/22% font limits and root text scaling. The three equal-width header cells use a base 8% and maximum 14% of board inner height after the row gap; grow only for measured title/supporting-copy height within this bound. Vertical brand columns keep their 75% target, 10% width floor and 18% frame-height budget. Balanced two-line candidates require a 15% size gain. Do not add template-name-specific title sizes. Mobile language UI-to-brand and brand-to-description share the 12px token, with independent 64px/48px ceilings at 342px inner width. PC/tablet one-page text chips move below a name only when the name alone fits one line and the chip makes it overflow; remeasure with fit/viewport changes. Mobile and multi-page keep their prior chip flow.
- Verify all affected mobile templates, narrow/wide phone widths and relevant locales, plus PC/tablet and excluded-engine regressions. Record actual DOM viewport dimensions, not just requested sizes; wait for the rendered layout/fonts to settle before claiming success.

- Customer content edits must invalidate fit caches even when IDs/counts stay the same. Recompute after visible copy, prices, images, widgets, fonts or viewport changes. Sale countdown ticks must not invalidate this content signature.

- Renderer responsibilities and extracted modules are documented in `docs/menu-template-architecture.md`. PC/tablet fit geometry and packing live in `one-page/pc-tablet/cafe-fit-engine.ts`; reusable types, pricing and content helpers live in `shared/cafe-template-*.ts` / `shared/cafe-menu-pricing.ts`. Keep existing mobile title/reading policy, multi-page engines and display engine independent. The user subsequently approved unifying Van Gogh/Sunday top-row outer padding and header-to-menu gap. Both inherit the shared responsive formula; do not reintroduce per-template PC inset or row-gap overrides.

- PC-only top-row rule: confirmed single-line text brands use 75% of the common header-to-menu base gap. Two/multiple lines, natural-wrap fallbacks, logos and all tablet/mobile devices keep the full gap. Compute title candidates from the unscaled gap to prevent feedback; restore the gap on text-brand unmount. Do not use template-name branches or change outer padding/header-height limits.

- ROSY ORANGE adaptive PC/tablet price leaders remain visible on every item, including chip-below rows. Reserve their readable minimum width when measuring chips; remove the old PC auto-start margin/short cap. Chips also move below if they add lines to an already long name. Four-menu-column rescue in a central-brand layout must account for all five actual columns and their gaps before checking the 12rem minimum. Leave mobile leader/chip flow and other engines unchanged.
